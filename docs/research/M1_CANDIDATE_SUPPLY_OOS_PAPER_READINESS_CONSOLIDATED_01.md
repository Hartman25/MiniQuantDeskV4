**Deduplication rulebook (written before any row was classified; the one amendment is disclosed below).**

Canonical spec per row (no inference; a missing element is recorded as missing): asset class, instrument universe, direction (`long`, `short`, `mirrored`, `hedged`, `long_short`), signal inputs, transforms with lookbacks, comparators, thresholds, entry rule, exit/hold rule, cadence, sizing, data requirements, state class (`bounded_history` vs `durable_state`), unresolved assumptions. A row without a computable rule is `INSUFFICIENTLY_SPECIFIED`; the reviewer never completes it.

| Novelty class | Rule (applied in this precedence; first match is primary, the rest listed as secondary relations) |
|---|---|
| `EXACT_DUPLICATE` | canonical spec equals an existing identity including every parameter |
| `PARAMETER_VARIANT` | same structure, only numeric parameters differ; **never a new hypothesis and never a trial by itself** |
| `MIRROR` | same signal, sign-flipped direction (identity-bearing; explicit `mirror_of`) |
| `COMPLEMENT` | the opposite state of an existing identity (for example short/flat when the other is long/flat; explicit link) |
| `COMPOSITE_OF_EXISTING` | conjunction, vote or sequence of two or more existing identities' signals |
| `SEMANTIC_VARIANT` | same economic mechanism, a materially different structural rule (extra filter, different exit, different input); needs a written difference |
| `GENUINELY_NEW` | no existing identity shares the mechanism |
| `UNKNOWN_NEEDS_REVIEW` | spec ambiguous; carries a written reason; never silently dropped |

Feasibility is a separate field: `M1_READY_SINGLE_SYMBOL_LONG_FLAT_DAILY_OHLCV`, `NEEDS_ADDITIONAL_AUTHORITATIVE_DATA`, `NEEDS_MULTI_SYMBOL_PORTFOLIO_ENGINE`, `NEEDS_SHORT_OR_BORROW_AUTHORITY`, `NEEDS_FUTURE_ASSET_SUPPORT`, `NEEDS_ML_OR_ALT_DATA_FRAMEWORK`, `INSUFFICIENTLY_SPECIFIED`. Result values, claimed returns, source marketing and the catalog's own `MQD_Priority`/`Implementation_Readiness`/`Source_Fidelity` never enter either field or any identity. EXT-023 and EXT-041 are flagged by the catalog itself as source-page mismatches and are `INSUFFICIENTLY_SPECIFIED` until the underlying article is read.

**Admission rule for the bounded population** (a rule, not a selection): a row is admitted to tier A iff (a) it has no feasibility blocker **and its native direction is a supported one, long-only or long/flat** (daily OHLCV and the session calendar only, one instrument at a time), (b) the rule is fully specified in the catalog text with no guessed parameter, and (c) novelty is `GENUINELY_NEW`. Tier B is the same but missing named definitions that an operator may declare prospectively. `SEMANTIC_VARIANT`, `COMPOSITE_OF_EXISTING`, `COMPLEMENT`, `MIRROR`, `PARAMETER_VARIANT` and `EXACT_DUPLICATE` are never admitted: they share the economic mechanism of a rejected or registered identity, which the mission excludes ("distinct from previously rejected economic hypotheses"). **Direction rule (independent review correction 01):** feasibility is `M1_READY` only when the authored blockers are empty *and* the native direction is `LONG_ONLY` or `LONG_FLAT`; any other direction (`LONG_SHORT`, `SHORT_ONLY`, `HEDGED`, `LONG_HEDGE`, `MARKET_NEUTRAL`, and the rotation, options, model, overlay and unknown directions) implies the blocker it requires. A symmetrical rule is not M1-ready until a separately specified, identity-distinct long/flat form exists (guardrail 4 of the catalog); no row specifies one. **Disclosure:** the earlier draft of the admission rule admitted `SEMANTIC_VARIANT` rows with a written material difference; it was tightened after the 200 rows had been read and before any was classified, on the mission's own distinctness criterion, not on any result. With the direction rule applied, the tightening has no effect on the population: EXT-179, the only row the looser rule would have added, is `LONG_SHORT` and is not M1-ready either way. Selection among admitted rows never uses returns, popularity or any score; if a cap K (OD-4) binds, ascending `EXT-nnn` breaks ties after keeping one representative per mechanism. Trials = admitted hypotheses x instruments (OD-2).

**Calendar-only analysis (`structural_reach.py`, native evaluation window 2016-03-01..2026-02-27, 2,514 sessions, 120 months; this is the declared-exposed development window, not out-of-sample evidence, and the Final Holdout beginning 2026-03-01 is excluded). Four kinds of statement, kept apart:**

1. *Exact calendar exposure* (from the pinned session calendar; no prices):

| Hypothesis | Sessions held | Exposure | Months holding | Months in which equity can change |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| EXT-032 scheduled holidays only | 186 | 7.4% | 89 | 96 |
| EXT-169 Santa Claus | 70 | 2.8% | 20 | 20 |
| EXT-044 monthly opex week | 586 | 23.3% | 120 | 120 |

"Months in which equity can change" are the months of the held sessions plus the month of the next-bar fill that closes each run; the backtest equity is cash plus mark-to-market with no interest or carry term, so a month with no position and no fill cannot rise.

2. *Established Promotion constraint (profitable months).* The evaluator takes the candidate's own continuous equity curve, keeps the last equity of each UTC month, and counts a month as profitable only if it is strictly above the previous month-end; every frozen declaration sets the minimum at 0.4. Consequences that follow from that accounting alone: the profitable-months fraction is at most the share of months in which equity can change (an upper bound, not a forecast): EXT-032 at most 79.8% (reachable in principle), EXT-044 at most 100%, **EXT-169 at most 16.8% over the evaluation window, and the 0.4 minimum cannot be reached on any single continuous equity curve covering more than six consecutive months** (Nov-Apr has 2 of 5 transitions able to rise, exactly 0.40; Nov-May has 2 of 6). EXT-169 is therefore unpromotable under the declared threshold whatever it earns. The other three are not excluded by this rule.

3. *Hypothetical illustration (not a requirement).* Alpha is `candidate_total_return_pct - benchmark_account_return_pct`; the benchmark buys the candidate's first resolved entry quantity at the candidate's first causal long-decision bar and holds it to the end of the bars under the candidate's own costs, while the candidate re-sizes at each entry. Whether alpha is non-negative therefore compares two realized profit-and-loss paths and depends on prices (if the passive holder's return is zero or negative the comparison is easy; a rule that earns its return in the held sessions can beat a diluted passive hold). Only under the assumptions that every session has the same price change, quantities are equal and costs are zero would a rule held a fraction e of sessions need 1/e times the passive per-session amount: 13.5x (EXT-032), 35.9x (EXT-169), 4.3x (EXT-044). These multiples illustrate dilution; they are not Promotion constraints and do not explain the Batch 03 alpha results.

4. *Genuine economic outcomes* (alpha, Sharpe, drawdown, profit factor, DSR, PBO, trades): require price data and are not computed or claimed.
